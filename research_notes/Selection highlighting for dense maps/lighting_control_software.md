# Selection rendering in lighting-control software (2D layout / plan views)

Scope: how professional consoles (ETC Eos, grandMA2/3, Onyx, MagicQ, Hog 4, Avolites Titan, QLC+, Lightkey, ETC Mosaic, MADRIX, Aputure Sidus Link Pro) and consumer/architectural systems (Philips Hue, Lutron, Casambi, Dynalite/DALI) show which fixtures are selected on a spatial layout, and how they physically "identify" (highlight/blink) the selected fixtures. The target is a web app showing ~110 fixtures on a floor plan, drawn in their live output colour.

Research note: several primary pages could not be read directly. ETC's Eos online-help Magic Sheet pages now return 404, ETC community threads return 403, Lightkey's PDF manual URLs return 404, and the ChamSys docs rate-limited and redirected. Where a claim rests only on a search-engine summary and not on a page I read, it is marked **[search-summary only]**.

## 1. ETC Eos family: Magic Sheets, Augment3d, tombstones, Highlight/Lowlight

### Takeaway
Eos keeps two channels of information apart. Selection is shown on an **outline** (gold/yellow in the channel displays, plus the outline layer of a Magic Sheet symbol). Live output (intensity and colour) is shown on the **fill/base** layer. Physical identification uses a separate mode: Highlight (`[High] [Enter]`). Selected channels go to full/open-white or to a highlight preset. Non-selected channels can drop to a Lowlight preset or to the Rem Dim level, and `[Next]/[Last]` steps through the selection one fixture at a time.

### Cited Findings
**Channel displays (Live/Blind tombstones), the long-standing Eos convention**
- ETC's support article lists the channel-number states as: "Patched Channel" (white), "Parked Channel", "Deleted Channel" (struck through), "Unpatched Channel" (gray), "Captured Channel" (highlighted), "Selected Channel" (outlined). — [ETC Support: Color and Text Indicators on Channels/Tombstones](https://support.etcconnect.com/ETC/Consoles/Eos_Family/Software_and_Programming/Color_and_Text_Indicators_on_Channels_Tombstones)
- Value colours on the same page: bright red = manual data needing storage; dark red = manual data from other user IDs; blue = intensity higher than the previous cue; green = lower; magenta = tracked; white = blocked; grey = default; yellow = from a submaster; gold = Staging Mode data; brown = temporary fader; pink = Augment3d Zone override. "When data is in transition (moving), it will appear in a lighter version of its color." — [ETC Support: Color and Text Indicators](https://support.etcconnect.com/ETC/Consoles/Eos_Family/Software_and_Programming/Color_and_Text_Indicators_on_Channels_Tombstones)
- "A gold outline indicates the channel is currently selected"; "a bright white number indicates the channel is parked, and a gold number indicates the channel is captured." **[search-summary only]**, attributed to the Eos help page "Indicators in Live and Blind", which now 404s — [Eos Online Help: Indicators in Live and Blind](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/04_System_Basics/04_Live_and_Blind_[Tab_1]/Indicators_in_Live_and_Blind.htm)

- In Live, the L2 workbook says: "Click on the Color Palette objects - changes color of fixtures – also notice channel objects are changing color … Roll down intensity wheel – channel objects slowly fade to black." — [ETC Eos Family L2 Enhanced Workbook v3.2 (PDF)](https://www.etcconnect.com/uploadedFiles/Main_Site/Documents/Public/Video_Tutorial/Eos_Family_L2_Enhanced_v3.2.pdf)

- The display tools include "Zoom to selection – zooms to show all selected objects" and "Center to selection – centers the display on the selected objects without changing the zoom". — [ETC Magic Sheets Expert Topics (PDF)](https://www.etcconnect.com/workarea/downloadasset.aspx?id=10737487527); [ControlBooth-hosted ETC Magic Sheets quick guide v2.0.1 (PDF)](https://www.controlbooth.com/attachments/eos_family_magic_sheets_v2-0-1reva-pdf.11104/)

Selecting from the left opens a green window and any object that crosses the boundary line … is selected." — [ControlBooth-hosted ETC Magic Sheets quick guide (PDF)](https://www.controlbooth.com/attachments/eos_family_magic_sheets_v2-0-1reva-pdf.11104/)
- The default colour of the Magic Sheet selection outline is reported inconsistently. One search summary said "channels will have a yellow outline when selected" and another said "orange outline". **[search-summary only; conflicting]** I could not read the primary Object Inspector page (404). — [Eos Online Help: Magic Sheet Object Inspector (dead link)](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/19_Magic_Sheets/Magic_Sheet_Editor/Magic_Sheet_Object_Inspector.htm)

**Highlight / Lowlight / Rem Dim (physical identification)**
- "To place Eos in highlight mode, press [High] [Enter]. The command line will show that highlight is currently in use." — [Eos Online Help: Highlight and Lowlight](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/08_Manual_Control/Highlight_and_Lowlight.htm)
- With no highlight preset, selected channels get: Intensity full; Focus no change; Color home; Shutter no change; Image home; Form no change. — [Eos Online Help: Highlight and Lowlight](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/08_Manual_Control/Highlight_and_Lowlight.htm)
- "When in highlight mode, [channel list] or [Group] [n] [Enter] [Next] will select the channel list or group and isolate the first channel." `[Next]` then cycles: the current channel goes to highlight while the others in the selection go to the lowlight preset, if one is defined. — [Eos Online Help: Highlight and Lowlight](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/08_Manual_Control/Highlight_and_Lowlight.htm); [ETC Support: Performing a Dimmer and Device Check with Highlight, Next, and Last](https://support.etcconnect.com/ETC/Consoles/Eos_Family/Software_and_Programming/Performing_a_Dimmer_and_Device_Check_with_Highlight%2C_Next%2C_and_Last)
- Rem Dim: "When enabled and highlight mode is active, the intensity for all non-selected channels are automatically set to the Rem Dim value." — [Eos Online Help: Highlight and Lowlight](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/08_Manual_Control/Highlight_and_Lowlight.htm)

### Inferences
- The Eos model maps directly onto a web floor plan: **fill = live output; outline/ring = selection**, and the two never share a channel. ETC's own advice when the outline is too thin is to make the selection mark bigger (a solid rectangle behind the symbol) rather than recolour the fill.
- Eos treats selected, captured and parked as separate states with separate marks: an outline for selected, a highlighted/gold number for captured, a bright-white number for parked. If the app has an "overridden/held" state as well as "selected", it should use a different glyph or text treatment, not a second outline colour.
- The partial highlight on Group Direct Selects is the Eos convention for a mixed selection at the group level. Each fixture carries its own mark, and group buttons show a full or partial state.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm from a primary page the exact default colour (yellow, gold or orange) or width of the Magic Sheet selection outline. The Object Inspector page is gone, and the training PDFs don't state it in their text layer.
- Eos shows selection-order numbers in the command line and the Live display, but I found no source saying Magic Sheets draw order numbers on symbols.
- No primary text describing exactly how Augment3d draws its yellow highlight (fixture body tint, outline or beam).

## 2. grandMA3 / grandMA2: Layout View, Selection Grid, Highlight/Lowlight/Solo

### Takeaway
MA's long-standing convention is **yellow = selected**: yellow fixture IDs in sheets, and a yellow frame around selected fixtures in the Layout View (can be toggled with "Show Selection"). **Red = programmer values**. Layout elements are themselves coloured by live output, with an optional dimmer bar, value text and border. The Selection Grid shows the whole selection as a green outline, with red centre lines. Highlight (Highlt key) sends selected fixtures to bright open white. Lowlight (MA+Highlt) sends a sub-selection to dim dark blue. Solo forces non-selected fixtures to dimmer 0. Next/Prev steps through the selection to confirm order.

### Cited Findings
- Layout View setting: "Show Selection: Displays the selected fixtures in a yellow frame in the layout view or not. Show Selection is not available in the Setup mode." — [grandMA3 2.5 manual: Layout View Settings](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_view_settings.html)
- Other Layout View settings: "Layer: Select the layer readout between DMX, Value, or Output"; "Selection Mode" (2D Grid or Linearize) controls how layout-selected fixtures map into the Selection Grid; grid style Off/Lines/Dots; grid colour. — [grandMA3 2.5: Layout View Settings](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_view_settings.html)
- In layout editing: "A yellow responsive frame appears around the selected items" (Auto tool: tap or lasso). — [grandMA3 2.5: Create Layout](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_create.html)
- Layout element display toggles: "Name", "ID", "CID", "Border", "Bar" (dimmer bar), "Value" (output value). There are also "Border Color" and "Border Size". "Selection Relevance" means that "the image colors with the For All or For Some color", i.e. a group/pool element shows whether all or only some of its fixtures are selected. — [grandMA3 2.5: Edit Layout Elements](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_elements_edit.html)
- grandMA3 system colours: "Yellow fixture names and IDs on a gray background indicate fixtures that are selected." "Red text with gray background" = values in the programmer that are not active; "White text with a red background" = active programmer values. — [grandMA3 2.5: System Colors](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/ws_colors_system.html)
- grandMA2 system colours (the long-standing convention): "Yellow fixture or channel IDs indicate fixtures or channels that are selected." "Gray fixture or channel IDs indicate fixtures or channel that are not selected." "Red values and white values indicate that this value is in the programmer." "Red background indicates that this value is in the programmer and that it is possible to store this value." — [grandMA2 help: System Colors](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA2/en/help/key_ws_colors_system.html)
- grandMA3 colour markers in the sheets: red = active absolute programmer values; mauve = relative; green = individual fade; orange = individual delay; purple = phaser; bright cyan = absolute preset values; dark cyan = relative preset; **blue = parked**; lavender/light pink = group-master influence. — [grandMA3 2.5: Color Markers](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/ws_colors_markers.html)
- Selection Grid visuals: the cursor cell is "the blue cell"; the grid origin is "marked with a white frame"; "The horizontal and vertical red lines represent the center of the selection"; "the green outline represents the overall selection"; with an Align Range enabled, "the frame around the selection … changes to a dark sea green color". — [grandMA3 2.5: Selection / Selection Grid](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/operate_selection.html)
- Highlight: toggled with the "Highlt" key (or `Highlight Off`). Selected fixtures go to their Highlight values, which "commonly include: Dimmer At 100%, Color At Open/White, Shutter At Open, Gobo At Open." Programmer values are not overwritten. — [grandMA3 2.5: Highlight, Lowlight and Solo](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html)
- Lowlight: "MA + Highlt" (only works while Highlight is active). The sub-selection goes to Lowlight values, typically "Dimmer At 20%, Color At dark blue." — [grandMA3 2.5: Highlight, Lowlight and Solo](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html)
- Solo: "Selected fixtures output normally while non-selected fixtures are temporarily suppressed (dimmer forced to 0)." — [grandMA3 2.5: Highlight, Lowlight and Solo](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html)
- The manual's workflow: press Highlt then "Next several times" to "visually confirm selection order, fixture orientation, or stage layout correctness". Highlight/Lowlight values are defined "in the Highlight column of the FixtureTypes Editor". — [grandMA3 2.5: Highlight, Lowlight and Solo](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html)

### Inferences
- MA's layout element puts several readouts on one symbol: a live-colour body, an optional border in a user colour, an optional dimmer bar and value text, and a separate **yellow selection frame** drawn outside. The selection mark has its own reserved colour (yellow), which is not used for any output state. That keeps it readable against live colour, except when the fixture itself is outputting yellow.
- The "For All / For Some" colouring is MA's explicit treatment of a mixed selection at the group level. Fixtures get per-fixture frames; group objects say "all" or "some".
- The Selection Grid's single green outline around the whole selection is the only "hull-like" group shape I found in MA. It is drawn in the abstract grid, not on the stage layout.

### Gaps
- No primary text on whether the layout's yellow selection frame is drawn per element or as one bounding frame (in Setup/edit mode it reads as one bounding frame around the items), nor on its thickness.
- I couldn't confirm whether the Layout View shows selection-order numbers on elements. The Selection Grid holds the order, and Highlight+Next is the documented way to check it physically.

## 3. Other pro consoles and software: Onyx, MagicQ, Hog 4, Avolites Titan, QLC+, Lightkey, ETC Mosaic, MADRIX, Aputure Sidus Link Pro

### Takeaway
Conventions vary by vendor. Some **recolour the fixture** for selection: Onyx bright green with the last-selected fixture in red; Mosaic red by default. Others **keep the fixture's live colour and add a separate indicator**: Titan's cyan programmer bar and corner glyphs; Sidus Link Pro's selection-order number badges and coloured status dots. Every system shows selection order somehow: order-touched semantics, numbered badges, or Next/Prev stepping. Nearly all ship a Highlight feature that drives selected fixtures to open white at full. Some step through the selection one fixture at a time (Hog Next/Back; Sidus Find Fixture, which flashes each light four times). MADRIX flashes the first pixel to show orientation.

### Cited Findings
**Obsidian Onyx**
- Fixture state colours: Grey = "Deselected fixture. It is in Programmer but will be unaffected by changes." Bright Green = "Selected fixture. When multiple fixtures are selected, all but the last selected fixture (shown in red) have this color." Red = "Last selected fixture. Only one fixture displays in red: this is the specific fixture represented in the Attribute Controls." Dark Green = "a fixture that was selected but has been overridden by the 'Next/Last' commands or the Fixture Selection Mask." — [Onyx Manual: Selecting Fixtures](https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/Fixtures_and_Groups/Selecting_Fixtures.htm)
- The 2D Plan's selection tools are Rectangle, Lasso, Line (draw a line through objects) and Path (flexible line). There is a "Highlight button" that replicates the console's highlight. "In Live mode, changes cannot be made to the 2D Plan and the fixtures will not show their output information such as intensity, color or pan & tilt." (This applies to the Live-vs-Edit distinction in that doc.) — [Onyx Manual: 2D Plan View](https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/2D_Plan_View/2D_Plan_View.htm)
- 2D Plan options: "Feedback" toggles parameter feedback (Intensity, Color, Pan & Tilt). "Beams" keeps feedback inside the fixture icon but hides beam drawings. ### Inferences
- Two families of selection rendering:
  - (a) **Recolour the fixture**: Onyx green, with red for the last-selected fixture; Mosaic red. This only works when the fixture glyph is *not* also showing live colour, or when feedback is turned off.
  - (b) **Overlay/adjacent mark** with the body left in live colour: MA's yellow frame, Eos's outline layer, Titan's cyan bar and glyphs, Sidus's number badges and dots.

  A floor plan whose fixtures are drawn in live output colour should use family (b). Family (a) would collide with output colours (a green-selected marker vs a green light).
- "Last-selected/primary" is a distinct state in Onyx (red) and MagicQ (single/sub-select). This matters if the app has a details panel showing one fixture.
- Order: Hog selects "in the order touched" with path/lasso. Sidus shows order badges. MagicQ shows order numbers top-right. Mosaic's lasso orders by number or by position. The app can show order as small numerals at the top right of each mark, MagicQ/Sidus style.
- MagicQ's edge-click row/column selection, and Hog's path tool and Onyx's line tool (both draw a line through fixtures), are good precedents for selecting runs of bulbs on a rail.

### Gaps
- No primary source on Onyx's 2D Plan selection mark (it presumably follows the green/red scheme, but the plan page doesn't say). A forum thread notes 2D plan colours were broken until the plan is ported to a new UI framework (ONYX-1230). — [Obsidian forum: 2D plan colors](https://forum.obsidiancontrol.com/t/2d-plan-colors/8959)
- No primary source on how MagicQ's Plan view, Hog's Plot or Titan's Layouts visually mark a *selected* (not merely programmed) fixture. The Titan docs mention only the cyan "in programmer" bar.
- No primary details for Lightkey's or QLC+'s selection colour or outline.

## 4. Consumer and architectural systems: Hue, Lutron, Casambi, LIFX, DALI/Dynalite

### Takeaway
Consumer and architectural apps barely have a multi-select layout view. Their main tool for answering "which one is this?" is **physical identification**: the light blinks, breathes or flashes. The Hue Entertainment-area map flashes the real lamp while you drag its icon. Casambi flashes each luminaire in turn during pairing. Lutron Designer's Verify Loads has a "Flash" action. Dynalite and DALI tools flash drivers or devices to locate them. Floor-plan control icons (Casambi Gallery) are simple circles that you tap to toggle or swipe to dim.

### Cited Findings
- Hue Entertainment area: users "tap an icon for each light and drag it to its approximate location within the room". **[search-summary of Philips Hue support FAQ]** — [Philips Hue support: Entertainment FAQ](https://philips-hue.com/ja-jp/support/topic/entertainment/100085)
- Several secondary guides say: "when you drag each light into position on the pretend 'map' of your entertainment area, the light in your room will flash so you know which one you are moving on the screen", and that when selecting lamps "the lights will blink so you know which lamp is which." **[search-summary of secondary guides]** — [Popular Science: How to sync Philips Hue](https://www.popsci.com/diy/philips-hue-sync/); [Hueblog: Set up Hue entertainment areas](https://hueblog.com/2019/10/17/set-up-hue-entertainment-areas-these-are-the-details-that-matter/) (fetched Hueblog page did not itself contain the blink detail)
- Hue Bridge identification mechanics: the v1 API `alert` values are "none", "select" (one short alert cycle) and "lselect" (alert cycles for 15 seconds). — [pkg.go.dev: haleyrc/hue constants](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/haleyrc/hue)
- A reverse-engineering thread reports that the Hue bridge sends a ZCL "Breathe" Trigger-Effect followed by "Breathe Stop" two seconds later, and that Philips lights "flash shortly in the last set color" (while deCONZ's Identify changed the colour to white). — [deCONZ REST plugin issue #183](https://github.com/dresden-elektronik/deconz-rest-plugin/issues/183)
- Hue CLIP v2 has a "breathe" alert/effect, which developers describe as similar to v1 `lselect`. **[search-summary]** — [openHAB: Philips Hue CLIP 2 discussion](https://community.openhab.org/t/philips-hue-clip-2-api-v2-discussion-thread/142111?page=6)
- Casambi pairing: when adding individually, "each luminaire will identify (flash) one by one and can be added to the network." **[search-summary of Casambi support]** — [Casambi Support: First time Casambi App use](https://support.casambi.com/support/solutions/articles/12000021612-first-time-casambi-app-use)
- Casambi Gallery (photo or floor-plan control): luminaires appear as "luminaire control icons (circles from which you can control a luminaire)", resizable by pinch. "If the required luminaire is in a group, first double tap the group and you can then select the required luminaire." — [Casambi Support: Gallery](https://support.casambi.com/support/solutions/articles/12000021614-gallery). Tap = on/off, side-to-side swipe = brightness, up/down = colour temperature; "you can … even [use] a floor plan". — [Casambi: Gallery functionality](https://casambi.com/news/app-gallery/)
- Lutron Designer (RadioRA 3/HomeWorks) Verify Loads: "The red section allows you to Flash the load and identify where the load is physically located"; "the Go To Level command can be used to specify a certain percentage"; "The blue section displays any error messages". This must be done on site. — [Lutron Support: Troubleshooting within Lutron Designer](https://support.lutron.com/us/es/product/radiora3/article/troubleshooting/Troubleshooting-within-Lutron-Designer)
- Lutron Designer+ (Vive) stamps each area of the floor plan with its solution and produces a CAD-exportable floor plan. This is a design and BOM tool, not a live-control selection view. — [LCA: Lutron Designer+ for Vive](https://lightingcontrolsassociation.org/lutron-introduces-the-next-generation-of-designer-for-vive/)
- Philips Dynalite System Builder: right-click a DALI controller, line or universe and choose Flash, or use "Flash and Stop Flash (F7)" in the Unassigned Devices editor, "to locate the drivers connected to the controller"; checkboxes in the Outputs editor "select and unselect … to locate the specified driver". — [Dynalite System Builder 4.41: Locating Drivers by Flashing](https://docs.dynalite.com/system-builder/4.41/signal_dimmers/addressable_config/locate_by_flashing.html)
- DALI: after an IDENTIFY DEVICE command, a device "outputs a visual signal (e.g. blinking or flashing) and/or an audio signal". Commissioning links a DALI address to a physical location "relative to a floor plan or a hierarchy (e.g., Building->Floor->Room->Name)". — [US11950344B2: Wireless commissioning system for DALI devices](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11950344)

### Inferences
- In consumer UIs the expected answer to "which light is this?" is a blink or breathe on the real lamp, started by touching or dragging its icon. In a pro UI it is a mark on screen plus an explicit Highlight mode. A web app for a real room should offer both: an on-screen mark and an "identify" pulse.
- Hue's identify ("breathe in the current colour") differs from pro Highlight (jump to open white at full). For lights that are already on and coloured, a brief breathe or flash in the current colour is less disruptive. Hard on/off bulbs can only "identify" by toggling.

### Gaps
- No primary Philips Hue page describing in-app selection marks on the Entertainment map, or a per-light "Identify" button in the current (2026) app.
- No sources found for LIFX's identify behaviour, Helvar Designer's or Tridonic masterCONFIGURATOR's floor-plan selection rendering, or Lutron's in-app (consumer) room views. These searches returned only marketing pages or patents.

## 5. Group shapes vs per-fixture marks, mixed selections, and legibility over live-colour fixtures

### Takeaway
Every system I found marks selection **per fixture**. No console draws a convex hull or blob around a selected group on the stage plot. Group membership and partial selection are shown on **group buttons/objects** instead: Eos Direct Selects highlight "partially", and MA's "For All / For Some" colouring does the same. The only group-level outline is MA's Selection Grid "green outline" around the overall selection, and it sits in an abstract grid view. Selection stays readable over live-colour fixtures because the mark lives on a **separate layer with a reserved colour**: an outline or frame (MA yellow, Eos gold/outline layer), a bar or badge (Titan cyan bar, Sidus number badges and dots), or text colour in sheets (MA yellow IDs). Several systems also let the user turn live feedback off, or make the mark bigger.

### Cited Findings
- Per-fixture frame: MA3 "Show Selection: Displays the selected fixtures in a yellow frame in the layout view". — [grandMA3 2.5: Layout View Settings](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_view_settings.html)
- Group partial state: Eos "Groups 2, 3, + 30 are partially highlighted as each contains part of the selection". — [ETC Eos L2 Workbook v3.2 (PDF)](https://www.etcconnect.com/uploadedFiles/Main_Site/Documents/Public/Video_Tutorial/Eos_Family_L2_Enhanced_v3.2.pdf)
- Group all/some state: MA3 layout element "Selection Relevance: … the image colors with the For All or For Some color". — [grandMA3 2.5: Edit Layout Elements](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/layout_elements_edit.html)
- Abstract-grid group outline: "the green outline represents the overall selection" and "red lines represent the center of the selection". — [grandMA3 2.5: Selection](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/operate_selection.html)
- Object groups on Eos Magic Sheets act as selection or zoom units (click one member to select or zoom to the whole object group). They are not drawn as a hull. — [ETC Magic Sheets Expert Topics (PDF)](https://www.etcconnect.com/workarea/downloadasset.aspx?id=10737487527)
- Separating the selection layer from the output layer: Eos `etc_symbol_outline` (selection) vs `etc_symbol_base` (fill colour/intensity link), and ETC's advice to "add another solid rectangle to the outline layer to show the selection better". — [ETC Support: Custom Magic Sheet Fixture Symbols](https://support.etcconnect.com/ETC/Consoles/Eos_Family/Software_and_Programming/Custom_Magic_Sheet_Fixture_Symbols)
- Separate indicator positions on a live-colour tile: Titan's cyan bottom bar (in programmer), tilde top-right (effect), fixture number top-left, intensity at the bottom, colour in the centre. — [Avolites Manual: Layouts](https://manual.avolites.com/docs/controlling-fixtures/layouts)
- Separate badges on a live-colour tile: Sidus Link Pro cells mirror the light's colour and brightness, with selection-order numbers and coloured status dots. — [Sidus Link Pro help: Layout View](https://help.aputure.com/en/sidus-link-pro/layout-view)
- Turning feedback off: the Onyx 2D Plan "Feedback" toggle (intensity, colour, pan/tilt) and "Beams" toggle; Onyx can also colour fixtures by Fixture Type Color / Fixture Item Color instead. — [Onyx Manual: 2D Plan Options](https://support.obsidiancontrol.com/Content/Onyx_Manual/Programming/2D_Plan_View/2D_Plan_Options.htm)
- Pushback on a bright overlay that hides output: an ETC user asks to disable Augment3d's yellow selection highlight because it hides the true look. **[search-summary]** — [ETC Community feature request](https://community.etcconnect.com/control_consoles/eos-family-consoles/i/feature-requests/how-to-disable-selected-highlight_in_yellow-function-in-augment3d)
- Using physical output rather than the screen: Solo (MA3: non-selected dimmer to 0) and Rem Dim (Eos: non-selected to the Rem Dim level) isolate the selection in the real rig. — [grandMA3 2.5: Highlight, Lowlight and Solo](https://help.malighting.com/grandMA3/2.5/HTML/highlight_lowlight_solo.html); [Eos Online Help: Highlight and Lowlight](https://www.etcconnect.com/WebDocs/Controls/EosFamilyOnlineHelp/en/Content/08_Manual_Control/Highlight_and_Lowlight.htm)

### Inferences
- For a 110-fixture floor plan with interleaved selected and unselected bulbs on the same rail, industry practice points to:
  - (1) a per-fixture **outline/ring drawn outside the live-colour body**, in one reserved colour not used for output state (MA yellow / Eos gold are the dominant conventions);
  - (2) optional small **order numerals** at a corner, MagicQ/Sidus style;
  - (3) group/tag chips that show **all / some / none** states instead of a hull on the plan;
  - (4) an **Identify/Highlight** action that pulses or flashes the real lights, stepping one at a time like Next/Back or Find Fixture when you need to confirm the order.
- A yellow/gold ring can disappear when the fixture is itself outputting warm white or amber, which is common in a room of incandescent-style bulbs. The professional tools I read don't address this directly. Precedents for working around it are ETC's "make the outline layer bigger/solid", MA's "Show Selection" plus a separate user "Border Color", and Onyx's "turn Feedback off". In practice the ring should contrast with the light (e.g. a dark gap or halo between the body and the ring) rather than rely on hue alone. (Inference; no source found that specifies a dual-stroke treatment.)
- Dimming unselected fixtures on screen is not a documented pro-console convention for the plot view itself. It does mirror the physical Lowlight/Solo/Rem Dim conventions, which do exactly this to the real rig. A "dim unselected" view mode would be a natural on-screen analogue (inference).

### Gaps
- I found no documented case of any lighting console or consumer app drawing convex hulls or blob outlines around a selected subset on a plan view. This may be because none exist or because docs don't describe it; video tutorials were not reviewed.
- I found no vendor guidance on ring thickness or contrast treatments (double stroke, dark halo) for selection marks over saturated or bright fixture colours.
